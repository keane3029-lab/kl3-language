Keane Language — third generation

Nobody has cracked KL3 yet.

KL3 is the third cipher in the Keane Language family — a hand-built letter-substitution system where every letter maps to its own 3-letter code, and the shifts don't follow a clean A→B→C pattern like KL2 did. That's on purpose. It's the reason it's still standing.

What makes it different

KL and KL2 both shift forward through the alphabet in order, so once you spot the rule, every letter falls. KL3 breaks that: codes jump around non-sequentially (R → TSR, Y → YZY), so pattern-matching from KL2 doesn't carry over. Two attempts have gone in so far. Both came back wrong.
