About KL3

KL3 is the third cipher in the Keane Language (KL) family, a constructed language project built by Keane.

The family so far

KL — the original, a 2-letter shift cipher (A→AB, B→BC...). Cracked by classmates.
KL2 — a 3-letter forward shift (A→ABC, B→BCD...), built by Keane himself. Also cracked.
KL3 — this one. Non-sequential 3-letter codes, no shortcut pattern. Still unsolved.
KL4 — in progress, not yet finished.

Who speaks it

  • Keane — creator of the whole KL family
  • Kier — attempted to crack KL3, unsuccessful so far
  • Sky — also builds their own constructed languages, attempted KL3, unsuccessful so far
  • Apheaea — active KL speaker

Why it's harder

KL and KL2 both shift in a predictable direction, so once someone notices the rule, the whole alphabet opens up. KL3 was built without that shortcut — the codes were chosen by hand rather than generated from a formula, which is exactly what's kept it standing at 0–2 against classmate attempts.
